Who we are

A school where every learner can find their way.

We bring together purposeful teaching, a caring community and a belief that education should help students understand the world — and their place in it.

Our story

Rooted in values. Ready for tomorrow.

EAK International School was imagined as a place where high expectations and genuine care belong together. We want students to leave school with strong knowledge, an open mind and the courage to contribute.

Our approach combines a clear academic foundation with opportunities to create, collaborate, reflect and grow into responsible global citizens.

  • Respect for every individual and perspective
  • Integrity in our choices and relationships
  • Curiosity that continues beyond the classroom
  • Responsibility to our community and environment

Academic journey

Learning that grows with you.

Our learning pathway is designed around students’ changing needs, strengths and ambitions.

Early years · Ages 3–6

Explore through play

Language, early numeracy, movement and imaginative play help young learners build confidence and connection.

Primary · Ages 6–11

Build strong foundations

Literacy, mathematics, science and the humanities develop alongside creative work and collaboration.

Middle school · Ages 11–14

Make meaningful connections

Students deepen subject knowledge, learn to research and communicate, and take greater ownership of progress.

Senior school · Ages 14–18

Shape the next chapter

Focused subject study, mentoring and leadership opportunities help students prepare for further study and life beyond school.

Dear Parents and Students,

Welcome to EAK International School, where every learner is encouraged to think independently, explore their potential, and grow with confidence.

How we teach

Active minds. Thoughtful teaching.
