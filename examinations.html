Before exam day

A few helpful reminders.

Simple routines can help students feel prepared, focused and ready to do their best.

◷

Be prepared

Plan ahead and arrive with enough time to settle in before the assessment begins.

  • Check the timetable the day before.
  • Bring permitted stationery and materials.
  • Follow the teacher's instructions.
✎

During the exam

Read every question carefully and use your time thoughtfully.

  • Write your name and details where required.
  • Manage time across all questions.
  • Raise your hand if you need assistance.
♡

Look after yourself

Healthy habits support learning and help you approach assessments calmly.

  • Get enough sleep and eat well.
  • Take short breaks while revising.
  • Speak to a teacher if you feel worried.
A broader view of progress

Assessment is more than a score.

At EAK, assessment can include written work, practical activities, projects and classroom participation. The aim is to understand progress, recognise strengths and identify where more support may help.
