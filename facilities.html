Explore the campus

Designed for the whole learner.

Our facilities support academic exploration, wellbeing, creativity and a strong sense of community.

Welcoming classrooms

Flexible learning spaces encourage conversation, collaboration and focused work.

Science & technology

Hands-on learning helps students test ideas, investigate and solve problems.

Library & reading

A space for stories, research, quiet focus and the joy of finding a new idea.

Sports & movement

Games and physical activity nurture teamwork, resilience and healthy habits.

Arts & performance

Students can practice, perform and express themselves through music, drama and visual arts.

Student support

Pastoral care and guidance help students feel safe, included and ready to learn.

School transportation

Organised transport routes and designated pickup points help make the daily school commute convenient for families.

Cafeteria & dining

A welcoming dining space gives students a place to enjoy their breaks, connect with friends and develop healthy eating habits.

Medical & first aid

A designated first-aid facility supports the initial response to minor injuries and sudden illness, with parents contacted when further care is needed.

  • Student clubs and interest groups

  • Community events and collaborative projects
