We want the admissions journey to feel clear, welcoming and personal. Here’s how to take the first step towards joining EAK International School.

The process

Five steps, with support at every stage.

Our team will guide families through each stage and help them understand what comes next.

Enquiry

Share your interest and ask questions about the school and year group.

Application

Complete the application details and provide the requested documents.

Interaction

Meet with the school; an age-appropriate assessment may be arranged.

Selection

The admissions team reviews the application and communicates next steps.

Enrollment

Confirm the place and complete joining formalities with the school office.

Before you apply

A few things to have ready.

Requirements can vary by year group. Use this checklist as a guide and confirm the current requirements with the admissions office.

  • Student’s birth certificate or age proof
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous school reports, where applicable
  • Transfer certificate, where applicable
  • Parent or guardian contact information
  • Any additional documents requested by the school

Admissions FAQs

Quick answers to common questions from families.

Families can send an enquiry throughout the year. Availability and application windows depend on the year group; please contact the admissions office for current information.

Campus visits can be arranged subject to availability. Contact the school office to ask about visiting times and any orientation sessions.

Depending on the year group, the process may include an age-appropriate interaction or assessment. The admissions team will explain the format in advance.

The checklist above is a general guide. The school will share the final document list and any year-group-specific requirements during the application process.
